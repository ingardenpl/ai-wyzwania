Cultural challenges of AI: regulation, language, creativity

Friday 27 November 2026, 12:45–14:15 (meeting IV) · Aula, Faculty of Law and Administration (WPiA), UMCS

Meeting plan

  • Regulation as a cultural choice: risk-based (EU), impact- and development-based (South Korea, Japan), market-driven (USA) and state-led (China) approaches.
  • Cognitive heuristics and game theory in the making of AI regulation.
  • Language and culture in training data: the dominance of English, Polish language models (PLLuM, Bielik), local values in global systems.
  • AI and creativity: machine creativity, generative art, deepfakes in culture.
  • Images of AI in culture: Lem, Asimov, ‘Blade Runner’, ‘Black Mirror’. Anthropomorphisation.
