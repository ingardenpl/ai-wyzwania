INVENTORY

Map legend

The aim of the lecture is to prepare future lawyers to critically assess the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on society, culture and the legal order, and to use AI tools in legal work in an informed way.

The course is a city. Each of the ten topics is a different district of Lublin in the year 2049; meetings II–V each cover one and a half topics. Visited districts are revealed, the next one flashes yellow and the rest are hidden in fog. Each district adds an item to your inventory. The map is decoration: all materials are also in the list below.

This is the English version of the course website. At present the syllabus, the assessment rules and district 01 are translated; the other districts link to the Polish materials.

Assessment

Three paths to choose from: A a test during the examination session, B a project of an application for lawyers (4–5 pages), C your own proposal agreed with the lecturer. You declare your choice by 6 November 2026 and submit your work by 18 February 2027. Pass threshold: 60%.
